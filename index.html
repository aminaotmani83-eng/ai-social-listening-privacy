This Privacy Policy explains how AI Social Listening Engine (“the Service”) handles information when an authorized business user connects Facebook Pages and professional Instagram accounts for social listening and comment analysis.

1. Information we process

  • Facebook Page IDs, Page names, and basic Page metadata.
  • Instagram professional account IDs, usernames, and basic account metadata.
  • Public comments and related interaction data made available by Meta, including comment text, post/media identifiers, timestamps, and commenter identifiers or usernames where provided.
  • OAuth authorization data and access tokens required to connect authorized accounts and make Meta API requests.
  • Workspace/account mappings used to associate connected social accounts with the correct organization.
  • Derived analysis such as language, sentiment, intent, topics, urgency, question/complaint flags, summaries, and suggested replies.

2. How we use information

  • Connect and identify social accounts that an authorized user chooses to link.
  • Receive eligible Facebook and Instagram webhook events.
  • Analyze comments and supported interactions for social-listening purposes.
  • Store analysis results for the authorized workspace.
  • Prevent duplicate processing and maintain account/workspace routing.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.

3. Meta platform data

The Service accesses Meta platform data only after an authorized user completes Meta’s authorization flow and grants the requested permissions. Users control which eligible Pages and Instagram professional accounts they authorize.

4. Access tokens and credentials

Access tokens and related credentials are used only to authenticate authorized requests to Meta services and maintain approved integrations. They are not sold or used for advertising. Access is restricted to systems and personnel necessary to operate the Service.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information or Meta platform data. Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers only as necessary to operate the Service, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.

6. Data retention

Connected-account information and social-listening records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, meet operational requirements, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or comply with applicable law. Data associated with a disconnected account may be deleted or anonymized when no longer required.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Choose which eligible Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts to connect.
  • Revoke the Service’s Meta access through Meta/Facebook account or business integration settings.
  • Request deletion of data associated with a connected account or workspace.
  • Request access, correction, or other privacy rights where applicable by law.

For deletion instructions, see the Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Children

The Service is intended for business and professional social-media account management and is not designed for use by children under 13.

10. International processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the user’s country. Where required, reasonable safeguards are used for international transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support/contact information listed for AI Social Listening Engine in its Meta application profile. A business may also provide its own support contact before public launch.

Important: Before a public commercial launch, review this policy against your final hosting, retention, security, and support processes and obtain legal review where appropriate.
