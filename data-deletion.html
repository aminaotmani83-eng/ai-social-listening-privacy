Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Privacy PolicyData Deletion

Users who have connected Facebook Pages or Instagram professional accounts to AI Social Listening Engine can request removal of their connected-account data and associated social-listening records.

Option 1 — Revoke Meta access

You can revoke the application’s access from your Meta/Facebook account or business integration settings. Revoking access prevents new authorized Meta data from being collected for that connection.

Option 2 — Request deletion

Submit a deletion request using the support/contact information listed for AI Social Listening Engine in its Meta application profile. Include enough information to identify the connected business workspace or social account, but do not send passwords, access tokens, or other secrets.

What we delete

  • Facebook Page and Instagram account mappings associated with the requesting workspace.
  • Stored comments or interaction records collected for that workspace.
  • Derived social-listening analysis associated with those records.
  • Stored authorization data that is no longer required after disconnection.

Timing

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days, unless a longer period is required or permitted by applicable law.

Verification

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the requester is authorized to act for the relevant connected account or workspace before deleting data.

Do not include passwords, Meta App Secrets, access tokens, or other authentication credentials in a deletion request.
